Statement fidelity
Statement797.f is the least universal palette for finite simple graphs on Fin n
with maximum degree at most d. Universally quantified n represents arbitrary
finite vertex sets by relabelling. It does not impose connectedness, nonempty
vertices, regularity, or any graph-size bound. Allowing unused colors correctly
means at most k colors, without requiring surjectivity onto Fin k.
The website's exact maximum degree convention is equivalent: adjoining a
disjoint star with maximum degree d permits reduction from <=d to =d, and a
coloring restricts back to the original graph. Degree zero consists of edgeless
graphs. This equivalence is a mathematical source justification, not an extra
proved theorem claimed in the local bridge.

Bridge correctness and nonvacuity
acyclicColorable_iff reconstructs the raw function predicate from bundled
Coloring: properness is intrinsic, while the cycle vertex changes only between
explicit and implicit binders. It preserves the quantification over every
IsCycle closed walk and two colors which may coincide.
f_eq proves equality of the complete palette-predicate sets under sInf; it does
not replace the extremal function by an upper approximation.
f_spec transports upstream extremalAcyclicNumber_spec, which follows from
Nat.sInf_mem using the explicit all-degree palette bound
64*(Nat.nthRoot 3 d+1)^4. Thus the natural infimum is nonvacuous for every d,
including d=0. No false/default-zero infimum is used to obtain the estimates.
The empty graph is harmless; nonempty edgeless graphs prevent zero available
colors from becoming a universal palette.

Real upper/lower bounds
Bounds797.upper_bound correctly casts the proved natural cube inequality,
then uses (d^(4/3))^3=d^4 and monotonicity of the cube on nonnegative values.
The constant is C=1024 and the assumption is d>=1.
The lower constant is c=(log(2)/2^67)^(1/3), strictly positive. For d at least
max(lowerD 1,2), log(d)>0 and the upstream cube lower bound applies. The
inequality Nat.log 2 d <= log(d)/log(2) enlarges the right-hand side of that
cube bound in the correct direction. Multiplication/division uses positive
log(2), log(d), and 2^67. Taking cube roots gives exactly
c*d^(4/3)/(log d)^(1/3) <= f(d). The common eventual threshold legitimately
combines this with the upper estimate; constants do not depend on d.
parts_i is precisely PR6340's real-power estimate after the proved f_eq.
parts_ii is precisely the affirmative little-o clause after f_eq. The True
biconditional represents the affirmative answer; no additional hypotheses
weaken the result. These statements retain the logarithmic gap and do not
assert an exact asymptotic equivalent or optimal constants. PR6340's separate
greedy and historical lower-bound variants are not extra verified endpoints
in this local bridge, nor are the paper's edge-coloring variants.

API SELF-REVIEW and repository scope
The repository has exactly the intended new module and one umbrella-import
change, with no edits to existing problem statements. The module defines
Coloring.IsAcyclic, AcyclicColorable, and ENat acyclicChromaticNumber. Infinity
represents absence of any finite palette. The proved finite-threshold iff,
non-top iff existence, comparison with chromaticNumber, and finite-graph witness
establish the claimed numeric semantics; no use of ENat.toNat hides infinity.
Injective recoloring preserves the predicate even for empty palettes; inside
a cycle the local color c(v) provides the required Nonempty witness. The finite
graph proof uses injective coloring and the fact that a genuine cycle has at
least three distinct supported vertices. No assumption forbidding ordinary
cycles or restricting to induced cycles is introduced.
